## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, athletic strength and telemetry tracking interface rooted in Nordic minimalism and calibrated laboratory equipment. Operating in an architectural light mode, it swaps stereotypical dark gym UI for warm, tactile mineral surfaces—oat, limestone, chalk, and raw granite—punctuated by high-salience kinetic lime and deep charcoal metrics.

### Personality & Tone
- **Tactile Laboratory Precision:** Evokes mechanical chronographs, surgical training diaries, and precision-milled aluminum equipment. Clean, objective, and unflinchingly functional.
- **Warm Mineral Substrate:** Natural stone, bleached canvas, and dry chalk white replace sterile blue-whites. The environment feels grounded, quiet, and disciplined.
- **Stoic Kinetic Energy:** High-intensity electric lime-neon accents guide the athlete’s focus strictly to live telemetry, completed sets, and micro-deltas without noisy gamification.

### Visual Style
A synthesis of **Nordic Craft Minimalism** and **High-Density Technical Functionalism**:
- Hairline structural micro-borders (`1px`) in warm stone charcoal.
- Generous structural gutters paired with dense, mono-spaced data tables.
- Zero blurry floating elevations; depth is communicated via subtle substrate layering, inset highlights, and sharp kinetic indicators.

## Colors

The color palette leverages an organic, warm-stone light mode architecture accented by high-contrast graphite typography and razor-sharp kinetic chartreuse.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#f8f7f4`):** The foundational backdrop. A warm, limestone-tinted off-white that reduces eye fatigue under harsh gym fluorescent lighting while preserving outdoor legibility.
- **Surface Sunken (`#f2f0eb`):** Applied to inactive input beds, recessed metric tracks, and secondary segmented controls.
- **Surface Card (`#ffffff`):** Pure chalk white, providing crisp elevated separation for primary lifting logs, charts, and metric clusters.
- **Surface Raised (`#eae7e0`):** Used for tactile active states, pressed button feedback, and pinned summary bars.

### Lines & Dividers
- **Subtle Border (`#e5e3dc`):** 1px structural dividing lines across table rows, tab bars, and module boundaries.
- **Strong Border (`#dbd8cf`):** Framing for active metric cards, focused inputs, and outer dialog perimeters.

### Functional Typography
- **Primary Type (`#111315`):** Razor-sharp, near-black graphite. Used for headline typography, load values, and critical telemetry readouts.
- **Secondary Type (`#575a5e`):** Muted stone-slate for metric units (`KG`, `LBS`, `RPE`), inactive row icons, and field headers.
- **Tertiary Type (`#8c8f94`):** Historical comparison values, disabled states, and auxiliary timestamps.

### Kinetic Accents
- **Kinetic Lime (`#c0eb1e`):** High-salience signal driver. Reserved for primary confirmation actions ("Complete Set", "Log Workout"), PR badges, active rest timer progress rings, and real-time velocity nodes.
- **Kinetic Lime Deep (`#9ec412`):** Border outlines and hover states for primary kinetic tokens in light environments.
- **Kinetic Lime Tint (`#f4fcd4`):** Soft luminous backdrops for active PR chips and highlighted exercise sets.
- **Alert Crimson (`#e53935`):** Reserved strictly for RPE 10 failure warnings, heart rate threshold limits, and destructive actions.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the disciplined geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**, the clinical readability of **Inter**, and the technical precision of **JetBrains Mono**.

### Structural Roles
- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Robust, athletic geometric sans-serif. Used with tight negative letter-spacing for program titles, session names, and block categorizations, projecting kinetic stability and craft.
- **Interface & Running Copy (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse. Applied to exercise notes, anatomical movement guides, coaching cues, and configuration lists.
- **Telemetry & Metric Indicators (JetBrains Mono):** Tabular, monospaced digits prevent visual shifting as numbers recalculate during active sets, bar-speed feeds, and rest countdowns.

### Typographic Rules
- All technical units (`KG`, `LBS`, `REPS`, `RPE`, `BPM`, `WATT`) must use `label-sm` in all-caps with `letterSpacing: 0.08em` and sit inline or stacked tightly beside metric values.
- Exercise log tables enforce strict vertical tabular alignment: weights, target reps, and actual reps line up cleanly on monospaced column baselines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a tight 4px structural baseline grid, balancing the high density required for multi-set workout logging with generous peripheral margins for rapid handheld scanning.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Mobile First Thumb-Zone Hierarchy:** Primary action triggers—such as "Log Set", "Next Exercise", and rest countdown overrides—remain anchored inside the bottom 35% of the viewport.
- **Fluid Micro-Grid:** Set logging tables maximize horizontal real estate. Column gaps are locked to `space-sm` (8px) on mobile viewports to allow full 4-column displays (Set, Previous, Load, Reps, Action) without horizontal scroll.
- **Desktop / Tablet Density:** At screen widths above 1024px, the layout transitions to a multi-column fixed dashboard: live workout execution column (60% width) flanked by real-time velocity curves and workout volume breakdowns (40% width).

### Ergonomics
- All interactive checkmarks, steppers, and plate calculator toggles maintain an absolute minimum touch boundary of `touch-target-min` (`44px` / `2.75rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects heavy blurry dropshadows and glossy skeuomorphism. Visual depth is derived from **tactile substrate tiers**, **crisp micro-borders**, and **focused kinetic glow rings**.

### Depth Layers
- **Floor Base (`#f8f7f4`):** Canvas base level. Grounded and flat.
- **Recessed Substrate (`#f2f0eb`):** Inset containers, data inputs, and segment background tracks. Layered with an internal border (`1px solid #e5e3dc`).
- **Elevated White Sheets (`#ffffff`):** Primary exercise cards, charts, and metric containers. Elevated strictly through hairline boundaries (`1px solid #e5e3dc`) and an ultra-diffused micro-shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(17, 19, 21, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(17, 19, 21, 0.02)`.
- **Kinetic Focus Ring:** Active timer pulses, currently executed sets, or newly achieved Personal Records activate a focused chartreuse halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #c0eb1e, 0 4px 16px rgba(192, 235, 30, 0.28)`.
- **Modals & Drawers:** Grounded in pure `#ffffff` with a structural top boundary of `1px solid #dbd8cf`. Backdrop overlay uses an opaque, dimmed mineral wash (`rgba(17, 19, 21, 0.45)`) paired with a sharp backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on disciplined, low-radius contours (`roundedness: 1`). The aesthetic conveys engineered durability, reflecting the cold edges of calibrated iron plates, barbell knurling frames, and precision timing instruments.

### Radius Distribution
- **Micro Radii (`2px` / `0.125rem`):** Tabular set check-markers, nested progress bar tracks, and segmented toggle thumbs.
- **Base Components (`4px` / `0.25rem`):** Primary action buttons, metric input fields, badge containers, and unit indicator pills.
- **Card Enclosures (`8px` / `0.5rem`):** Exercise log blocks, velocity curve graphs, and workout summary cards.
- **Drawers & Bottom Sheets (`12px` / `0.75rem`):** Applied exclusively to the top left and top right shoulders of incoming mobile sheets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Action ("Complete Set", "Start Workout"):** Solid Kinetic Lime background (`#c0eb1e`) with deep graphite text (`#111315`), styled in `jetbrainsMono` uppercase with 600 weight. Border is `1px solid #9ec412`. On active press, applies immediate tactile compression (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Graphite Button:** Deep graphite background (`#111315`) with chalk white text (`#ffffff`), `4px` corner radius.
- **Tertiary Substrate Button:** Mineral surface (`#ffffff`), `1px solid #dbd8cf` border, with `#111315` text. Hover state warms to `#f2f0eb`.

### Set Logging Rows
- Standardized into a high-density 5-zone grid:
  1. `SET` index in `label-sm` (`#8c8f94`).
  2. `PREVIOUS` benchmark in `metric-sm` (`#575a5e`).
  3. `WEIGHT` input field inside a sunken `#f2f0eb` container.
  4. `REPS` input field inside a sunken `#f2f0eb` container.
  5. `CHECK` action button (`32x32px`).
- **Completed State:** Row shifts subtly to `#fcfbf8` with a 1px border highlight of `#c0eb1e`. Inputs lock into monospaced text with high-contrast `#111315`.

### Checkboxes & Confirmation Gates
- `28x28px` square with `4px` radius.
- **Default State:** Recessed `#f2f0eb` fill, `1px solid #dbd8cf` border.
- **Confirmed State:** Vivid `#c0eb1e` fill with a `2.5px` thick `#111315` check glyph.

### Metric Cards & Telemetry Blocks
- Pure white container (`#ffffff`) framed by `1px solid #e5e3dc`.
- Upper label: `label-sm` all-caps in `#575a5e`.
- Center value: `metric-xl` in `#111315` flanked by superscript unit labels (`KG`, `RPE`).
- Micro-footer: Delta pill showing comparison against previous week (`+2.5%` inside `#f4fcd4` with text `#586e06`).

### Input Fields (Weight & Reps)
- Fixed numeric entry boxes. Background `#f2f0eb`, border `1px solid transparent`, text styled in `metric-md` (`#111315`).
- Active focus state: Background switches to `#ffffff`, border switches to `1px solid #111315`, paired with a crisp `2px` kinetic ring (`#c0eb1e`).

### Chips & Badges
- Precision micro-tags with `2px 8px` padding and `4px` radius:
  - **PR Record Badge:** Background `#f4fcd4`, text `#4d6100`, border `1px solid #c0eb1e`.
  - **Target Zone Chip:** Background `#f2f0eb`, text `#575a5e`, border `1px solid #e5e3dc`.
  - **Overload Warning:** Background `#fde8e8`, text `#c5221f`, border `1px solid #fad2d2`.
