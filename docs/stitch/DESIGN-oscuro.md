## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance strength and athletic conditioning interface that harmonizes **Nordic Athletic Craft** with **Cyber Kinetic Precision**. The aesthetic bridges the raw, deliberate physicality of heavy barbell work and natural outdoor training with the clinical, telemetry-driven rigor of elite sports science.

### Personality & Tone
- **Unyielding & Grounded:** Earthbound, organic minerals, granite weight stacks, and chalky textures meet clean negative space.
- **Razor-Sharp Precision:** High-cadence micro-indicators, technical data feeds, and laser-accurate metrics instill confidence and discipline.
- **Stoic Dynamism:** Uncluttered layouts that resist gym-bro gamification in favor of purposeful, quiet strength punctuated by high-voltage kinetic feedback.

### Emotional Target
The athlete feels disciplined, focused, and powerful. Interaction feedback is swift and mechanical, evoking calibrated laboratory instrumentation wrapped in warm, tactile Nordic minimalism.

## Colors

The palette operates on a foundation of warm deep slate-stone and mineral charcoal, accented by tactile oat and bone hues, illuminated by focused kinetic volt.

### Color Tiers & Intent

- **Kinetic Volt (`#D4FF32`):** The primary signal driver. Reserved exclusively for high-salience moments: PRs (Personal Records), active tracking sessions, progress delta badges, rest timer completion, and live telemetry nodes. It is used with extreme discipline to avoid sensory fatigue.
- **Nordic Oat & Bone (`#E6E1D6` / `#D8D2C4`):** The secondary anchor. Provides organic warmth for primary headlines, structural weights, set totals, and resting states. Counterbalances the coldness of standard dark telemetry modes.
- **Mineral Slate (`#7B828E`):** The tertiary bridge. Used for sub-labels, inactive axis markers, unit descriptors (kg/lbs/reps), and secondary metric legends.
- **Charcoal & Deep Slate-Stone (`#141517` / `#1C1E22` / `#25282E`):**
  - Surface Ground (`#141517`): Pure canvas background, reducing visual strain in dimly lit lifting stations.
  - Surface Substrate (`#1C1E22`): Primary card, set log, and drawer background.
  - Surface Elevation (`#25282E`): Active input row, raised telemetry modules, and segmented switch tracks.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Success/Kinetic PR: `#D4FF32` (Volt)
  - Critical/RPE 10 Overload: `#FF4A4A` (Crimson Flare)
  - Recovery/Stasis: `#4ECDC4` (Glacial Teal)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy relies on a trifecta of deliberate roles:
1. **Syne (Display & Titles):** Unapologetically architectural, sculptural, and organic. Brings editorial prestige and physical heft to session names, lift milestones, and phase markers.
2. **Hanken Grotesk (Interface & Body):** High-clarity neo-grotesque optimized for rapid scanning in motion. Free of idiosyncrasies, ensuring quick legibility when an athlete is under heavy load.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Metrics & Telemetry):** Strict, monospaced tabular data. Numbers align reliably down workout logs, rep grids, velocity readouts (m/s), and rest countdowns without layout twitching.

### Application Notes
- All metric values must pair unit tokens (`KG`, `LBS`, `RPE`, `MS-1`) set in `label-sm` with tabular alignment enabled.
- Workout and phase titles use uppercase tracking via `headline-md` or `headline-sm` with tight vertical metrics to keep vertical screen footprint compact on mobile devices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a mobile-first, high-density 4px baseline rhythm engineered for one-handed operation during intense physical activity.

### Grid & Ergonomics
- **Mobile Container:** Full fluid width bounded with `16px` (`1rem`) outer margins.
- **Card Padding Hierarchy:**
  - Compact Set Table: `10px 12px` to maximize screen data density.
  - Telemetry & Analytics Cards: `16px 20px` to let graphs and key figures breathe.
- **Thumb Zone Compliance:** Primary interactive triggers (Log Set, Rest Timer Adjust, Plate Math toggle) reside strictly in the bottom 40% viewport quadrant.
- **Minimum Hit States:** All actionable touch points (set checkboxes, increment buttons, plate selectors) adhere to `touch-target-min` (`44px` / `2.75rem`), preventing miss-clicks with chalked or shaking hands.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating drop shadows and blur gimmicks that dilute tactical focus. Depth is expressed through **tactile substrate stepping**, **low-contrast stone borders**, and **subtle kinetic luminescent rings**.

### Depth Layers
- **Floor (Canvas):** `#141517` — completely non-reflective, flat base.
- **Substrate 1 (Cards & Panels):** `#1C1E22` paired with a 1px structural hairline border (`rgba(230, 225, 214, 0.08)`). Provides crisp structural separation without aggressive contrast.
- **Substrate 2 (Interactive Elements & Set Rows):** `#25282E` with an inset highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Kinetic Flare (Active Telemetry Focus):** When a timer is active, an exercise is in-flight, or a set breaks a PR, the surface triggers an ultra-subtle volt aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -6px rgba(212, 255, 50, 0.18)`.
- **Modals & Bottom Drawers:** Grounded in `#1C1E22` with a top perimeter border of `1px solid rgba(212, 255, 50, 0.3)`. Backdrop employs a dark dimming wash (`rgba(10, 11, 12, 0.85)`) without heavy blur.

## Shapes

The geometry draws from precision engineering: controlled, disciplined radii that convey architectural weight rather than toy-like softness.

### Corner Configuration
- **Base Geometry (`roundedness: 1`):** Core interactive inputs, metric badges, and set cells apply a `4px` (`0.25rem`) radius.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg`):** Main performance containers, workout blocks, and graph modules utilize `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- **Surface Panels & Modals (`rounded-xl`):** Bottom sheets and slide-over workout drawers utilize `12px` (`0.75rem`) at upper boundaries.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted strictly to active session status tags, battery/connection indicators, and rest-timer progress bars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Action (e.g., "Complete Set", "Finish Workout"):** Background `#D4FF32`, label text `#141517` (`jetbrainsMono`, 600 weight, all caps). Instant tactile snap response with no squishy ease-in curves (`transition: transform 60ms linear; active: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Slate Button:** `#25282E` container with `1px solid rgba(230, 225, 214, 0.14)`, text `#E6E1D6`.
- **Destructive / Discard Action:** Transparent container, `#7B828E` text, transitioning to `#FF4A4A` on hold.

### Telemetry Cards
- Containers styled with `#1C1E22` fill and `1px solid rgba(230, 225, 214, 0.07)`.
- Header houses category label in `label-sm` (`#7B828E`) alongside an optional kinetic status dot (4px, glowing `#D4FF32`).
- Content displays metrics in `metric-xl` (`#E6E1D6`), appended with inline small-caps units (`#7B828E`).

### Set Logging Rows
- Composed of 4 columns: `SET NO`, `PREV`, `WEIGHT`, `REPS`, followed by a tactile confirmation check.
- Alternating subtle row zebra shading using `#1C1E22` and `#212328`.
- Completed state shifts the entire row border to a crisp `1px solid rgba(212, 255, 50, 0.35)` with values shifting to high-contrast white.

### Metric Badges & Chips
- Monospaced, high-density chips with `4px` radius and `2px 8px` internal padding.
- Neutral Chip: Background `rgba(230, 225, 214, 0.06)`, text `#D8D2C4`.
- PR Kinetic Chip: Background `rgba(212, 255, 50, 0.12)`, text `#D4FF32`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 255, 50, 0.3)`.

### Form Inputs (Weight & Rep Entry)
- Numerical-only keyboards default.
- Background `#141517` inset within `#25282E`, text in `metric-md` `#E6E1D6`.
- Focus state activates an outline of `1px solid #D4FF32` with an ambient glow of `rgba(212, 255, 50, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes (Set Confirmation)
- Square-profile `28x28px` box, `4px` corner radius.
- Inactive: Background `#25282E`, border `1px solid #7B828E`.
- Active / Checked: Background `#D4FF32`, check icon `#141517` (drawn with thick, sharp `2.5px` stroke).
